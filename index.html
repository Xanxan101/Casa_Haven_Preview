How booking works

  1. Send your inquiry — your dates and guest count.
  2. Get a quote — we will send an email with pricing and a GCash QR code; your dates are softly held for 24 hours.
  3. Pay to confirm — reply to the email with your full payment or 50% downpayment, and reference number of receipt.
  4. You're booked — we verify the payment and your dates are locked in.

No payment within 24 hours automatically releases the hold.

🏡 Casa Haven – Booking Terms & Conditions

To secure and confirm your preferred dates, please take note of the following:

💳 Reservation & Payment

  • A minimum 50% downpayment is required to reserve and hold the selected dates.
  • The remaining balance must be paid before or upon check-in, as agreed with Casa Haven.
  • A booking is considered confirmed only after the required downpayment has been received and verified.

📅 Date Reservation

  • Once payment has been received, the selected dates will be reserved exclusively for the guest.
  • Casa Haven may release unpaid or partially secured reservations if the required payment is not received within the agreed timeframe.

❌ Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • The downpayment is non-refundable once the booking has been confirmed.
  • If the guest cancels the reservation, the downpayment will not be refunded.
  • Date changes may be considered depending on availability and must be requested in advance.
  • Any approved date change may be subject to applicable rate differences.

⚠️ Important

  • Please make sure that your selected dates and number of guests are correct before making the payment.
  • Payments made to secure a reservation are considered an agreement to these booking terms.

🤍 Thank you for choosing Casa Haven. We appreciate your understanding and look forward to hosting you!
